## Findings, most severe first

### 1. A hidden terrain still takes clicks (Confirmed)

`src/scene/Terrain.tsx:348-363`. Switching the Terrain layer off sets `visible={false}` on the group, but the mesh keeps its `onClick`. Neither R3F's `intersect` (`events-e51d7528.esm.js:675`) nor three's `Raycaster` (only `layers.test`) skips invisible objects.

- Repro: 3D map, Terrain off, click where the model was. The Inspector shows a full reading for ground that is not drawn; in Measure mode the clicks set A and B.
- Same path also stops a click from reaching a finding badge behind the invisible surface (see #7).

### 2. The Inspector can list a finding while saying "no significant change" (Confirmed, reproduced)

`src/compliance/inspect.ts:99-130` against `:121, :146`. The change verdict uses the bilinear sample between cell centres (`sampleBilinear`); the finding list uses the nearest cell (`cellAt`). The engine classifies whole cells. So a point inside a flagged cell but off its centre gets the finding without the change that caused it.

- Repro (probe, 10 m cells, one cell lowered 1 m): at the cell centre `change: 'excavated'`, `dh = -1`, findings `[R1-1]`; 4 m east, still in the cell, `change: 'none'`, `dh = -0.60`, findings `[R1-1]`. The panel says "No significant change (threshold ±0.64 m)" and links a boundary violation.
- `inspect.test.ts:76` only reads cell centres, where the two agree, so the suite cannot see it.
- Fix direction: take the verdict from the engine's cell (`change.excavated/filled[index]`), or index findings by the same reading.

### 3. Escape on the phone drawer also clears the measurement; map shortcuts act behind the scrim (Confirmed)

`src/ui/pages/MapPage.tsx:69-82` and `src/ui/shell/AppShell.tsx:49-53`. Both listen for `keydown` on `window`. AppShell calls `preventDefault()` on Escape, but MapPage never checks `e.defaultPrevented`, and a focused nav link is not an input, so its typing guard does not apply.

- Repro: 3D map below `lg`, measure A and B, open Menu, press Escape. The drawer closes and the measurement is gone.
- While the drawer is open, `[`, `]`, `m`, `f` and `r` also change the map hidden behind it.

### 4. The coordinate form keeps a stale status message (Confirmed)

`src/ui/panels/CoordinateEntry.tsx:40-47`. When the pick changes, the form resets its fields but not `status`, which clears only when the visitor types.

- Repro: enter E 5000, N 5000 → "…outside the survey grid… elevations cannot be." Then click the terrain. The fields and panel show the new on-grid point; the live region still says it is outside the grid. `m` or Clear behave the same.

### 5. Rounded figures contradict the verdict printed beside them (Confirmed)

- `src/ui/panels/InspectorPanel.tsx:87` vs `:96-100`: the Change row rounds to one decimal, the verdict quotes the threshold to two. A change of −0.645 m reads "−0.6 ± 0.07 m" next to "Excavated: lowered by more than 0.64 m".
- `src/ui/home/NoiseCheck.tsx:11` with `:149-156`: `m()` drops to one decimal from 1 m up. With the "Global elevation models" preset (σ1 = σ2 = 4) the band is 11.31 m and the threshold 11.81 m; the change slider (step 0.01, max 12) reaches 11.32, which prints "11.3 m is larger than the ±11.3 m…", and 11.80 prints "11.8 m … below the 11.8 m R1/R3 threshold".

### 6. "Show on map" on the map's own Findings tab breaks Back for a shared link (Confirmed, traced)

`src/ui/panels/FindingsPanel.tsx:86-89`, `src/ui/route.ts:67-69`, `src/ui/pages/MapPage.tsx:39-56`. On `#/monitor?finding=R1-1` the hash is not exactly `#/monitor`, so `navigate('monitor')` assigns `location.hash` and pushes an entry; the effect then `replaceState`s it to `?finding=R1-2`.

- Repro: open `#/monitor?finding=R1-1`, click Show on map on R1-2, press Back. The address reads `?finding=R1-1`, R1-2 stays selected: neither effect re-runs (`[ready, findings]`, `[selectedFinding, ready]`). What is shared no longer matches the screen.

### 7. The terrain takes clicks aimed at a finding badge drawn through it (Traced; how often is Plausible)

`src/scene/Overlays.tsx:283-296` against `Terrain.tsx:355-361`. Badges render with `depthTest={false}`, so they show through pit walls, but R3F delivers the click to the nearest hit first and the terrain handler calls `e.stopPropagation()`.

- Repro: Oblique or Pit preset, a pin low in the pit (R2-1 at the sump) with its badge behind the near wall. Clicking the badge reads a point on the wall instead of selecting R2-1.

### 8. The landing date bar labels an in-between model as a dated survey (Confirmed, low)

`src/ui/home/StoryClock.tsx:26-28`. During the 900 ms tween between Baseline and Current it reads "<date>, day N of 281". The 2026-10-18 log says a line called the blend "an illustration, not a survey"; that text no longer exists anywhere in `src/`. Not shown under reduced motion.

### 9. A shared link with a second `?` loses its finding (Confirmed, low)

`src/ui/route.ts:47`: `hash.split('?')[1]` keeps only the text between the first and second `?`. `#/monitor?ref=x?finding=R1-1` opens with no finding. Links made by `findingLink` never hit this; hand-edited or tracking-tagged ones do. Fix direction: slice from the first `?`.

### 10. Changing exaggeration during a camera flight is lost (Traced, low)

`src/scene/CameraRig.tsx:127-137` against `:141-158`. The flight's poses are fixed at the old exaggeration; the rescale effect updates `c.target.y` and `lastEx`, then the next flight frame overwrites the target. Move the slider 1.5× → 3× within the 1.3 s Pit flight (or the 2.6 s intro) and the camera settles on the old floor depth.

### 11. A texture still in use can be disposed (Traced, low)

`src/scene/Terrain.tsx:305-314`. One effect disposes `texture`, `texture2` and the materials when any of them changes; if only `morphTo` changes, `texture` is disposed while the new materials still use it. three.js re-uploads it, so the cost is a wasted upload, not a visual fault. Practically reachable only by a reload or retry that replaces one survey.

### 12. Latent: the ramp builder throws if its first point is rejected (Confirmed latent)

`src/data/synthetic.ts:172-182`. If the fold check rejects the first ramp point, `points` is empty and `end.x` throws inside the worker, failing the whole survey. Both shipped pits build, so nothing breaks today; it would surface if `PIT_SEED` or a pit outline changes.

## Plausible, not proven

- `src/geo/grid.ts:25-36`: `cellAt` accepts points up to half a cell beyond the outer centres, where `sampleBilinear` returns null. An edge-cell finding would be listed beside "outside the survey grid". No shipped finding sits on an edge cell.
- `src/geo/sitePlan.ts:282`: a contour exactly at the surface minimum (`level <= min`) is skipped. Survey noise makes an exact tie unlikely.
- `src/ui/pages/FindingsPage.tsx:49`: the CSV object URL is revoked synchronously after `a.click()`; some browsers have cancelled downloads that way. The CSV browser check passes in Chromium.
- `src/state/story.ts` (`tweenStory`): its rAF keeps running after the landing page unmounts, so leaving and returning within 900 ms of pressing Baseline can overwrite the mount reset.
- Honesty rule: finding area, volume and max excess (Findings page and tab, the PDF figures line, Overview "Removed outside the lease") and the map measure chip (`MapHud.tsx:111-113`) carry no ±. Whether AGENTS.md's "± uncertainty on values" covers derived aggregates is the owner's call.
